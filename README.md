\# YatraIndia



YatraIndia is a tourism and travel platform for discovering destinations,

booking hotels and cabs, managing travel services, and connecting customers

with verified travel partners.



\## Planned Modules



\- Authentication

\- User Management

\- Destinations

\- Hotels

\- Cabs

\- Bookings

\- Partners

\- Payments

\- Notifications

\- Reviews

\- Offers

\- Advertisements

\- Administration
